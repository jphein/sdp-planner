# sdp-planner

A free, browser-only plan tracker for California Self-Determination Program (SDP) families. Drop in your spending plan and your FMS year-to-date report; see your year-end forecast by service code, plan who bills what with a live what-if calculator, and print a proposal. Nothing leaves your browser.

Status: under construction (2026-09-30). See `docs/SPEC.md`.
