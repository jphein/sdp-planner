    § 01

    Proposal — who bills what this year

    One line per provider. Every field is yours: name, code, service, rate, start month, hours per week, per month or per year, and dollars; they stay in sync (hours/wk × weeks from the start month to year end = hours/yr). Billed so far is what has actually posted (amber = running ahead of that line's pace) and never changes the proposal. ⤓ fills a line to exactly what is left in its code, ✕ removes it, Undo steps back, and you can save named proposals to compare. Budget lives by service code: money never moves between codes.
